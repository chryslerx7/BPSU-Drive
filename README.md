# BPSU Drive

**BPSU Drive** is an Android-based campus service request and payment verification system designed for students and staff of Bataan Peninsula State University (BPSU).

The application allows students to submit requests for campus services, track request progress, and submit official receipt photos for payment verification. Staff members can review requests, verify receipts, and process service requests.

> **Project Status:** UI/UX implementation completed for the Student side. Staff UI and application logic are planned for subsequent development phases.

---

## 📱 Overview

BPSU Drive aims to provide a centralized and user-friendly way for students to request university services without relying entirely on manual processes.

The system is designed around a simple workflow:

```text
Student Login
     ↓
Browse Services
     ↓
Create Service Request
     ↓
Payment at Cashier
     ↓
Take Receipt Photo
     ↓
Submit Receipt
     ↓
Staff Verifies Receipt
     ↓
Request Accepted
     ↓
Processing
     ↓
Completed
